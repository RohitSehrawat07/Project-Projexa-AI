Built for Competitive Excellence

♟
Live ELO Ranking
Dynamic ratings that rise and fall with every quiz. Five tiers — Bronze to Diamond — give students a living, honest grade.
⚡
Daily Challenges
One question every day. Correct answers boost ELO. Streak milestones unlock badges and bonuses.
🤖
AI Prediction
Weighted ML model analyzes accuracy, streaks, and speed to forecast At Risk, Average, Good, or Excellent outcomes.
🔬
Match Review
Post-quiz breakdowns show per-question time, class comparisons, and pinpoint academic Blunders and Great Moves.
⚔
Head-to-Head
Challenge peers directly. ELO adjusts by opponent strength — fair, social, and highly motivating.
📊
Teacher Dashboard
Rating heatmaps and automated at-risk alerts give educators real-time intelligence to intervene early.
