How It Works

Simple for teachers.
Engaging for students.

A workflow designed to add gamification to your existing teaching — without replacing it.

🏫
01

Teacher creates a class

Set up your class with your institution name. A unique join code is generated automatically.

→
📝
02

Create quizzes

Build assessments from the question bank across easy, medium, and hard difficulty levels.

→
🔗
03

Students join online

Students sign up and enter the join code to access their class environment from any device.

→
⚔️
04

Features

Everything in one platform

For Students

Learn through competition

⏱️
Timed Quizzes
Ranked assessments that affect your ELO rating based on difficulty and accuracy.
♟️
ELO Rating System
Dynamic ratings from Bronze to Diamond. Fair, skill-based, and motivating.
🏆
Class Leaderboards
See where you stand in your class. Challenge the students ranked above you.
⚡
Daily Challenges
One question per day. Correct answers boost ELO. Build streaks for bonus rewards.
🥊
Tournaments
Round-robin competitions between classmates with ELO stakes and champion badges.
📈
Progress Tracking
Track your accuracy, speed, streak, and ELO over time. See your improvement clearly.
For Teachers

Analytics that actually help

🏫
Class Management
Create classes, generate join codes, and manage your students in one place.
✏️
Quiz Creation
Build quizzes from the question bank or create custom assessments for your class.
⚡
Automatic Results
Quizzes are auto-graded. No manual checking. ELO updates happen in real time.
📊
Performance Analytics
Class-wide accuracy, individual student stats, and trend data in your dashboard.
🚨
At-Risk Alerts
AI-based predictions identify students likely to fall behind so you can intervene early.
🎯
Class Leaderboard View
See the full class ELO ranking, tier distribution, and quiz completion rates at a glance.
Why EduRank

Built for real educational outcomes

🎮

Engagement through competition

ELO ratings, tiers, and leaderboards create a competitive environment that motivates students to keep learning — not just to pass, but to improve their rank.

📏

Measurable progress

ELO is a proven rating system used in chess and other competitive domains. It gives a clear, honest signal of where each student stands and how they're improving.

🔍

Actionable analytics

Teachers see not just scores, but patterns — which students are consistent, which are declining, and which need attention before they fall too far behind.

🏗️

Simple to deploy

No complex integrations. Teachers sign up, create a class, share a join code. Students are up and competing within minutes — no training required.
